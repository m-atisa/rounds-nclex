## Process
1. **Integration.** Six authors inventoried about 330 slide and handout items. They filled every gap in the existing lessons and wrote the six new exemplar lessons in the site's plain-language format: key points, key idea per part, common traps, an NCLEX walkthrough, a worked case, a North Carolina practice note, and course sources.
2. **Questions.** About 42 NCLEX-level questions were written for each new exemplar (252), plus 35 gap-filling questions for Modules 42, 44 and 49. Every slide bullet is tested at least once. All questions pass `scripts/validate-questions.mjs`.
3. **Independent review.** 15 reviewers re-checked all six lesson files against the slides and the prior version, and all nine new question files against the slides and the North Carolina facts. Fixes included ambiguous answer keys, giveaway cues, and outdated statements.
4. **Course vs. current.** Where a slide is outdated, the lesson says "Your course materials say X. Current standard: Y." Examples: OPTN naming; capacity vs. competency; the Patient Bill of Rights replaced by the Patient Care Partnership (2003); FDA-approved and state-licensed marijuana products moved to Schedule III (DEA final order, April 28, 2026; other marijuana stays Schedule I); the HIPAA preexisting-condition rules superseded by the ACA; and reports going to the agency (DSS), not only to a supervisor.
