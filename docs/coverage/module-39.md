- **Course vs. current disagreements:** 3, each stated in the lesson as "Your course guide says X. Current standard: Y..." and listed in "Course source & clinical clarification":
  1. Only California mandates hospital ratios (14 states regulate staffing). Current: Oregon passed hospital ratios in 2023 (phased in from 2024); Massachusetts mandates ICU ratios.
  2. Connect families to aid "before lifetime insurance caps are exceeded." Current: the ACA bars most plans from lifetime dollar limits on essential benefits.
  3. U.S. has the "highest" obesity and suicide rates and "lowest" life expectancy. Current: these rankings are among high-income peer countries (Commonwealth Fund), not all countries.
- **Flashcards:** 21 added (30 → 51). Every PDF glossary term now has a flashcard with `topic` set.

They cover SNF evaluation, ethics/veracity, the staffing evidence, Ranier (2), Montoya, the oxygenation cases (2), the U.S. paradox, cost-containment strategies, and socialized medicine.
- **Learning outcomes:** the PDF has no separate learning-outcome list. The 18 lesson objectives were used instead. Each one has 3 or more questions (counts below).
