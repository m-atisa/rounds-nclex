# Module 49: Legal Issues — coverage audit

Source: Module_49_Legal_Issues_Study_Guide-v2 (pp. 2825–2834). Site files: `content/guides-m49-pro.js`, `content/m49-pro.js`, `content/xq-m49-1.js`.

## Summary

- **Items inventoried:** 140 (7 learning outcomes, 29 key terms, every table/box row, every list item, safety alerts, Clinical Example A, and the top 10 traps).
- **Already fully covered:** 69.
- **Missing or incomplete before, now added or corrected:** 63. A further 8 rows were covered but carry a course-vs-current note (9 rows in total carry one).
- **Course-vs-current disagreements noted in lessons and in “Course source & clinical clarification”:** 9 rows / 9 distinct points:
  1. Privacy “protected under the Fourth Amendment” — it limits government searches; in health care, HIPAA/state law/privacy tort apply.
  2. Statute of limitations 1–2 years — NC is generally 3 years with a 4-year repose.
  3. CSA enforced by the Drug Enforcement “Agency” — correct name is Administration.
  4. Schedule V “no Rx required” — many Schedule V drugs (e.g., pregabalin) need a prescription.
  5. Ezogabine (Schedule V example) — discontinued 2017.
  6. Schedule I marijuana — federal rescheduling under review since 2024.
  7. Box 49.2 state counts — dated; state laws (especially abortion) changed after 2022.
  8. Failure to report child abuse “may result in civil charges” — in NC and many states it is a crime.
  9. Whistleblower Protection Act of 1989 — covers federal employees; most nurses rely on OSHA provisions and state laws.
- **Lesson changes:** 5 new sections (“Rights, responsibilities, and why policies exist”; “How legal issues link to other concepts”; “Four strategies to prevent negligence”; “Worked case: Marvin’s choice (Clinical Example A)”; “Your course’s top 10 exam traps”), full Table 49.1, full Box 49.1, full Table 49.2, full concepts table, six-elements definition table, abuse-signs table, and 4 new key terms.
- **Flashcards:** 17 added (32 → 49); every master-checklist key term now has a flashcard.
- **Questions:** 6 added (m49-x1-095 to m49-x1-100); every learning outcome now has at least 3 questions.
- Validation: vg.mjs OK, vq.mjs OK, m49-pro.js parses.
