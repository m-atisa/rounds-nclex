# Module 37: Collaboration — coverage audit

Source: *Module 37 Collaboration Comprehensive Study Guide* (course pp. 2553–2563; PDF pages cited below).
Site files: `content/guides-m37-pro.js`, `content/m37-pro.js`, `content/xq-m37-1.js`.

## Summary

- **Items inventoried:** 111
- **Already fully covered:** 49 (some had small details or citations added)
- **Added or completed (missing or incomplete before):** 57
- **Corrected:** 2
- **Course-vs-current notes:** 3
- **New lesson sections (6):** The six QSEN competencies and Box 37.1 in full; Table 37.1 in full; How does collaboration link to other concepts?; Worked case: Mr. Hainsley (Example A); Evidence box: nurse–physician barriers and fixes in full; Worked case: Mr. Eliot (Example B). Sections per topic now: 15 / 13 / 11.
- **New flashcards:** 22 (now 56). Every PDF key term has a card with `topic` set.
- **New questions:** m37-x1-085 to m37-x1-091 (7). LO 37.2 had no direct items and now has 3 (085–087). Every LO has at least 3 questions.
- **Disagreements, all stated in the lessons and in “Course source & clinical clarification”:**
  1. NLN name: the course says “National League for Nurses.” The correct name is National League for Nursing.
  2. ANA collaboration standard: the course says Standard 10 (2015). The 2021 edition makes it Standard 11. Expect 10 on the course exam.
  3. Box 37.2: the course calls it “four domains” but lists five. Learn all five.
  4. RD credential: the course says “board-certified,” with a bachelor’s or master’s degree. The Commission on Dietetic Registration gives the credential, and new candidates have needed a graduate degree since January 1, 2024.
  5. A small fix: the site said “about 90%.” It now uses the course’s figure of 89.9%.
  6. Already noted before this audit: IPEC updated its competencies in 2023 with the same four domains; “THE — Background” in I PASS THE BATON; the course’s “at the very least” wording for compromising.
- Validators: `vg.mjs` OK, `vq.mjs` OK, and `m37-pro.js` parses.
