# Unit 7 coverage: Module 44 Ethics (slides 1–11 + three handouts)

**Scope:** Ethics PowerPoint (Heather Wilson, BSN, RN) slides 1–11 with speaker notes and SmartArt; handouts "Further Illustrations for ETHICAL PRINCIPLES", "Ethics Letter to Students" (Sandra H. Carr, MN, ARNP), and "A Verbatim Account of Ethical Dilemma" (Chitty, 2005, pp. 546–547). Slides 12–20 (Exemplars 44.B and 44.C) are out of scope; they are covered by their own lessons (pointer added in the values-codes course-source section).

**Summary:** 62 teachable items were inventoried. 38 were already taught. 24 gaps were filled in the existing topics. Values-codes gained one section; ethical-principles gained one section plus additions to two others; morality gained two sections. In morality, the two overlapping moral-distress sections were merged into one, with every fact kept. Changes also include 23 new flashcards in `m44-pro.js` and 15 new questions in `xq-m44-u7.js` (5 SATA, 4 priority). All validators pass: vg OK, with each lesson at or under 5,000 words, and vq OK.

## Learning-outcome question coverage (Module 44 bank: m44-pro.js, xq-m44-1.js, xq-m44-u7.js)
The Ethics PowerPoint has no explicit outcomes slide, so each slide's main teaching point was treated as an outcome:
- Ethics vs morality, values, core values, codes (s2–4): many existing items, e.g. m44-pro-002/004/008/009/012/014 and m44-x1-012/014/019/021/024.
- Principles and their examples (s5 + handout): existing items plus m44-u7-010 to -013.
- Strategies (s6): 13 or more existing items (mentor and ethics committee).
- Theories (s8): m44-pro-030/031/041 and m44-x1-070/071/080.
- Moral development (s9): m44-u7-001, -002, -003 (new).
- Moral principles (s10): 7 or more existing fidelity and accountability items.
- Nursing process (s11): m44-u7-003 to -007 (new) and m44-x1-028.
- End-of-life double effect: m44-u7-008, -009 and m44-x1-062.
- Ethics letter: m44-u7-014, -015 and m44-x1-037/-063.
