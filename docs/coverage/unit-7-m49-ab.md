## Summary

- **Items inventoried:** 49 (40 slide items from slides 2 and 11–21; 7 related study-guide and Module 42 items; 2 NC items). Slides 11–21 have no [Notes] or [SmartArt].
- **Gaps:** none of slides 11–21 had a full lesson before. Two new exemplar lessons now teach every item.
  - `licensure-regulation` — "49.A Nurse Practice Acts": 14 sections, ~4,160 words.
  - `advance-directives` — "49.B Advance Directives": 14 sections, ~3,860 words.
- **Flashcards:** 28 new (15 for 49.A, 13 for 49.B). Every slide key term has one. No questions written.
- **Course-vs-current notes (3):**
  1. Slide 13: “BON administers the NCLEX.” The NCSBN develops it and a vendor delivers it; the Board decides eligibility and grants the license.
  2. Slide 16: “Credentialing = the state license.” Credentialing is the broader process of verifying qualifications; licensure is the legal part.
  3. Slide 21 (clarification): “State may appoint a surrogate.” State law sets the surrogate order, usually with no court action; a court appoints a guardian only when required or disputed.
  - Also an NC naming note: “Durable Power of Attorney for Healthcare” = NC Health Care Power of Attorney.
