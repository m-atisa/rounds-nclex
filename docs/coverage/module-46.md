- Questions: outcome 46.6 had only about 1 direct question. Added m46-x1-089 to m46-x1-092 (advocacy, quality improvement, legal/ethics/safety/informatics SATA, health policy). Every outcome now has at least 3 questions.
- Disagreements: ACEs "1 in 6 children"; "pressure ulcer"; ACA mandate "repeal"; $10,739 per-capita figure; team leader "delegates" to licensed staff.
