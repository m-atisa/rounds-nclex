**Course-vs-current notes (4):**
1. Slide 8 lists the ANA nursing-process standards as five steps. ANA lists six, adding outcomes identification.
2. Slide 26 says reports go through the chain of command. The duty is met only when the report reaches the designated agency (NC: county DSS).
3. Slide 10 says Good Samaritan laws protect health care workers. The NC law protects any person giving unpaid emergency care.
4. Slide 10 defines a whistleblower as reporting "unethical" practices. Legal protection usually needs a violation of a law or rule.

Clarification (not outdated): slide 3 lists loss of license as a criminal-law result. Courts impose fines and prison; the Board acts on the license.
