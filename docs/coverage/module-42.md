## Summary

- **PDF items inventoried:** 67. These are 5 glossary terms, the 7-area accountability statement, 5 components plus their sub-claims, 6 expanded duties, 7 matrix rows, 3 exemplar learning outcomes, 3 core areas, Benner’s 5 stages, 3 Safety Alert items, 3 diversity items, 3 case studies with all 11 questions, and 4 synthesis links.
- **Already fully covered:** 23.
- **Added or completed in the lessons:** 36. The main gaps were the full p. 2705 related-concepts matrix (only the comfort row was partly covered) and the Miller and Epworth cases worked question by question. Other gaps: the Miller collaborators list, EI’s “emotional appraisal” and relationship role, lifelong-competence sources, the exact Safety Alert phrases, the MRC tasks, and the ANA “primary commitment” link.
- **Course-vs-current notes:** 8 items. Each is stated in the lesson as “Your course guide says X. Current standard: Y. On a course exam… on the NCLEX…”. Each is also listed in that topic’s “Course source & clinical clarification” section:
  1. TOL is for “BSN or higher” vs every RN.
  2. MSN and DNP/PhD listed as entry routes.
  3. Codes of ethics “regulated … via the NCSBN” vs written by the ANA.
  4. “Magnet requires BSN-prepared nurses” vs required for managers and leaders.
  5. Comfort assessed “at least once per shift” vs reassessed after each intervention.
  6. Mandatory reporting of “children and older adults” vs state-specific adult rules (in NC, disabled adults).
  7. The RN “delegates” straining to an LPN vs the 2019 NCSBN/ANA term “assignment.”
  8. Tyree must “refuse” vs decline the independent assignment before report and negotiate a safe role.

  - accountability-practice: “What does the related-concepts matrix say?”, “Worked case: Ms. Miller’s hidden pain”, and “Worked case: Mr. Epworth’s kidney stone” (11 → 14 sections).
  - competence: “Why must competence last a whole career?” and “Worked case: Tyree Campbell floats to the NICU” (11 → 13 sections).
  - accountability-foundations: still 11 sections, with expanded content.
- **Flashcards:** all 5 PDF key terms already had cards. 9 cards were added (44 total): two course-vs-current cards, the 7 matrix concepts, the trauma and teaching implications, the Miller collaborators, the exemplar learning outcomes, situational learning, and lifelong-competence sources.
