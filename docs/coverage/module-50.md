## Summary

- **PDF items inventoried:** 97
- **Already covered on site:** 61. Some of these also got new flashcards or questions.
- **Added (missing or incomplete before):** 30
- **Corrected (site was less accurate or less complete than the PDF):** 2
- **Course-vs-current notes:** 4. Each one is stated in the lesson and listed in "Course source & clinical clarification".
  - Sentinel event definition. Course: "…serious physical or psychological injury, or the risk thereof (including loss of limb or function)." Current Joint Commission: a safety event that reaches the client and causes death, permanent harm, or severe temporary harm.
  - 72-hour window. Course: abduction, rape, assault, homicide, or suicide "in facility or within 72 hours of discharge." Current: the 72-hour window applies only to suicide. The other events count when they happen on site.
  - TQM origin. Course: "invented by Shewhart, made famous by Deming." Current history: Shewhart built the tools and PDCA. Deming and others popularized TQM.
  - Utilization review. Course: "required by Medicaid and The Joint Commission." Current: CMS Conditions of Participation require it for Medicare and Medicaid hospitals, and Joint Commission standards include it.
  - Minor: To Err Is Human is dated 2000 in the course (the book). The report came out in late 1999.
