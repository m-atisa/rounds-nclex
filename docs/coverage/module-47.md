# Module 47: Health Policy — coverage audit

**Source:** Module_47_Health_Policy_Exam_Study_Guide (9 pages, textbook pp. 2795–2804)
**Site files:** content/guides-m47-pro.js, content/m47-pro.js, content/xq-m47-1.js, content/xq-m47-2.js

## Summary

- **PDF items inventoried:** 115, plus 1 row on question coverage.
- **Already fully covered:** 63
- **Missing or incomplete before, now added:** 46 (mostly missing detail: "11 divisions", OEMS certification and program accreditation, NPSG 07.06.01, the Outcome 47.3 concept table, TJC settings, full accreditor level lists, association purposes and focus areas, Medicaid eligible groups, ESI union and usual-and-customary detail, and Medicare/Medicaid key terms).
- **New lesson sections:** "Worked case: CAUTI policy and the surveyor" (Clinical Example B) and "Worked case: the isolated nurse anesthetist" (Clinical Example C). There is also a new Outcome 47.3 table in policy-process.
- **New flashcards:** 13. Every PDF key term now has its own card: executive branch agency, law, regulation, HMO, PPO, Medicare, Medicaid, Medigap, PCP. There are also cards for Figure 47.1, Outcome 47.3, NPSG 07.06.01, and OEMS.
- **New questions:** m47-x1-070 to m47-x1-072, which strengthen Outcome 47.3. Every outcome, 47.1 to 47.7, now has at least 3 questions.
- **Disagreements (6), each stated in the lesson as "Your course guide says… Current… course exam / NCLEX" and listed in the Course source sections:**
  1. NPSGs (2003, yearly): hospital NPSGs became National Performance Goals on Jan 1, 2026.
  2. "TJC requires reporting of sentinel events": TJC requires a response (root cause analysis and action plan). Self-reporting to TJC is voluntary.
  3. OSHA covers "private- and public-sector workers": state and local public workers are covered only under OSHA-approved state plans.
  4. "American Association of Nurse Anesthetists": renamed American Association of Nurse Anesthesiology in 2021 (still AANA).
  5. Medigap letters "designated by state laws": the lettered plans are standardized by federal law (MA, MN, WI differ).
  6. Part B "20% co-payment": it is 20% coinsurance.
  - Minor: the guide says "16 terms" but lists 17. All 17 are covered.
- **Validation:** vg.mjs OK, vq.mjs OK, and m47-pro.js parses.
