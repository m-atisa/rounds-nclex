## Summary

- **Items inventoried:** 91
- **Already covered:** 47 (46 fully, 1 expanded)
- **Missing or incomplete before, now fixed:** 44 (23 added, 16 corrected/completed, 5 course-vs-current notes)
- **New lesson sections (5):** Which laws protect clients’ rights?, How do nurses advocate for children and families? (full Table 43-1), How does advocacy link to other concepts? (full 3-column matrix), Worked case: Lois Potter, and Worked case: Donna Taylor (in advocate-role). Also Worked case: Heather Adams (reporting). The William Johnson section is now a worked case with a Q&A table.
- **New flashcards (7):** patient advocacy, championing social justice, vulnerable populations, ADA, the 7 related concepts, Donna Taylor, Table 43-1.
- **New questions (6):** m43-x1-087 to m43-x1-092. They cover LO 43.3, which had only about 3 items before. Each outcome now has at least 3 items (43.3 has 9; Exemplar 43.A has 6).
- **Disagreements (7, each stated in the lesson and in the “Course source & clinical clarification” section):**
  1. The guide says the PSDA was enacted in 1991. It was passed in 1990 and took effect December 1, 1991.
  2. The guide says the ADA was amended January 1, 2009. The ADA Amendments Act was signed in 2008 and took effect January 1, 2009.
  3. The guide says “State Protection and Advisory Systems (PAIMI).” The correct name is the Protection and Advocacy for Individuals with Mental Illness program.
  4. The guide says “Psychological Health Center for Excellence.” The correct name is Psychological Health Center **of** Excellence.
  5. The guide says “Disability Rights of Ohio.” The correct name is Disability Rights Ohio.
  6. The guide says the nurse may NOT legally serve as surrogate or DPOA. The client’s own nurse must never be the surrogate. Off duty, the law does not bar a nurse from this role, but it is a conflict best avoided.
  7. Heather Adams: the guide says a nurse cannot be an ethical surrogate. The law does not bar it. The conflict is strongest when the nurse is also her clinician. The advice is the same.
