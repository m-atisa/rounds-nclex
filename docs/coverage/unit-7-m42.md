# Unit 7 coverage: Module 42 (Accountability and Exemplar 42.A Competence)

Sources: Unit 7 instructor decks *Module 42 Accountability* (19 slides) and *Module 42 Ex. A Competence* (9 slides). These were checked against the existing site lessons, which were built from the Module 42 PDF study guide (pp. 2703–2712).
Site files edited: `content/guides-m42-pro.js` (lessons), `content/m42-pro.js` (flashcards), and new `content/xq-m42-u7.js` (13 questions).

## Summary

- **Items inventoried:** 64 slide items, counting each bullet, list, and SmartArt node grouped by idea. No [Notes] appear in either deck.
- **Already covered:** about 18. These are mostly the five components, the related concepts, Benner, the first rule of competence, and problem solving with EI.
- **Gaps filled:** about 45. The big gaps were three concept learning outcomes the PDF never covered: standards of care and national guidance (slides 3–6), socialization (LO 42.3, slides 11–12), and factors influencing practice (LO 42.4, slides 13–19). Smaller gaps were the slide wording of definitions, accountability for trainees, QSEN, the slide component grouping, and the slide 7 continuing-learning list. Two more were “accountable for own choices even when collaborating” and the history of competence (“formerly care and comfort”).
- **Lesson changes:**
  - accountability-foundations: 11 → 14 sections. New sections: “What is socialization to nursing?”, “What forces shape nursing practice?”, and “How do laws and staffing shape nursing?”. “Who sets the rules and standards?” was expanded to cover standards of care, TJC, ANA, and NLN. The lesson is now 5,000 words, so some duplicate inline notes were shortened (the full notes stay in the last section).
  - competence: 13 → 14 sections. New section: “How do technology and telehealth change care?”. Slide wording was added to four existing sections.
  - accountability-practice: slide 10 and slide 4 citations added.
  - All three lessons pass vg.mjs.
- **Flashcards:** 38 new cards (44 → 82). Every slide key term now has a card.
- **Questions:** 13 new items, m42-u7-001 to m42-u7-013. There are 4 SATA, 4 priority, and 5 single-answer items, and they pass vq.mjs. Learning-outcome coverage across all m42 files:
  - LO 42.3 (socialization) had 0 items and now has 3.
  - LO 42.4 (factors) had 1 item and now has 8.
  - LO 42.1 and 42.2 already had many items.
  - The exemplar outcomes already had many items; lifelong competence gains m42-u7-012 and m42-u7-013.
- **Course-vs-current notes added:** 3.
  1. Slide 15 “Electronic Health Report Systems” → electronic health record (EHR).
  2. Slide 17 PSDA “all competent adults” → every adult client of a Medicare- or Medicaid-funded facility. If the client is incapacitated, the information goes to family or a surrogate, then to the client once able. A directive is never required for care.
  3. Slide 3’s list of TJC, ANA, and NLN as standard-setters was clarified: TJC standards apply to organizations, the ANA writes nursing standards, and the NLN focuses on education. A note that shortage projections vary was also added.
- **Existing notes:** the entry-degree note (slide 8 repeats the PDF list) and the code-of-ethics note still apply.
